# Newspaper Advertising Proposal

## 1. Executive Summary

This proposal presents a simulated newspaper advertising campaign for GreenNest Developers, a fictional residential real estate company in Bengaluru.

The objective is to generate relevant property enquiries and encourage prospective buyers to schedule site visits.

**Important:** This is a sample portfolio proposal, not an official BCCL media plan or quotation.

## 2. Campaign Objectives

- Increase awareness of the residential project among potential homebuyers.
- Generate enquiries from relevant target locations.
- Encourage qualified prospects to book property site visits.
- Measure campaign responses using trackable contact details.

## 3. Target Audience

- **Audience:** People actively considering buying a home.
- **Geography:** Selected Bengaluru neighbourhoods, to be confirmed with the client.
- **Interest:** Residential properties that match the project's location and price range.
- **Message:** Project benefits, location advantages, and a clear call to action.

## 4. Sample Media Plan

The following is an illustrative planning model. Actual newspaper names, editions, inventory, and rates must be verified with the publisher.

| Campaign Element | Proposed Plan |
|---|---|
| Campaign duration | Four weeks |

| Geographic focus | Selected Bengaluru editions |
| Creative approach | Property benefits and enquiry-focused message |
| Response tracking | Dedicated phone number or QR code |
| Evaluation | Enquiries, qualified leads, and site visits |

## 6. Creative Concept

**Headline:** Your Next Chapter Begins at Home

**Supporting message:** Discover a home designed around your everyday needs. Explore the location, amenities, and available configurations.

**Call to action:** Request project details or book a site visit today.

The developer's actual project details, claims, contact information, and approvals must be verified before publication.

## 7. Measuring Campaign Performance

Track the following:

- Total enquiries received
- Qualified leads
- Site visits booked
- Site visits completed
- Cost per lead

**Formula:**

Cost per lead = Total campaign cost ÷ Number of attributed leads

For example, if the campaign costs ₹2,00,000 and generates 100 attributable leads, the illustrative cost per lead is ₹2,000.

This is a hypothetical calculation, not a forecast or actual campaign result.

## 8. Post-Campaign Review

After the campaign, compare results against the objectives agreed with the client. Review lead quality, site visits, and cost per lead before recommending whether to repeat, revise, or expand the campaign.
