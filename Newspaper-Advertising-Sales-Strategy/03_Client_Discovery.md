# Client Discovery & Needs Analysis

## 1. Project Scenario

This is a simulated sales conversation with GreenNest Developers, a fictional Bengaluru-based residential real estate developer.

The purpose is to understand the client's marketing objectives before recommending a newspaper advertising campaign.

## 2. Client Discovery Questions

| Area | Question | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| Business objective | What is the main goal of your upcoming campaign? | Identifies the business outcome |
| Project details | Which property or project do you want to promote? | Defines the campaign focus |
| Target audience | Who is the ideal buyer by location, age group, and budget? | Helps select relevant newspaper editions |
| Geography | Which Bengaluru locations are most important? | Helps plan local reach |
| Timeline | When will the campaign need to run? | Helps schedule advertising |
| Budget | What budget range has been approved? | Keeps the proposal realistic |
| Current marketing | Which channels are you currently using? | Identifies the role of print advertising |
| Success measurement | How will you measure campaign performance? | Establishes measurable outcomes |

## 3. Simulated Client Brief

**All details below are illustrative assumptions, not actual client information.**

- Client: GreenNest Developers (fictional)
- Industry: Residential real estate
- Location: Bengaluru
- Campaign objective: Generate qualified enquiries for a residential project
- Target audience: Prospective homebuyers researching properties in selected Bengaluru locations
- Proposed campaign period: Four weeks
- Budget: ₹2,00,000, used only as an example
- Success measures: Enquiries, qualified leads, site visits, and cost per lead

## 4. Initial Advertising Recommendation

Subject to validating the client's audience and budget, consider a targeted newspaper advertising test.

Potential elements:
- Select newspaper editions based on the target buyer's geography.
- Use a clear property headline and key project benefits.
- Include a dedicated phone number, QR code, or landing-page link to track responses.
- Test one creative approach before expanding the campaign.
- Review enquiry quality and site visits, not just the number of responses.

Actual newspaper selection, ad sizes, rates, dates, and availability must be confirmed with the publisher.

## 5. Proposed Campaign KPIs

| KPI | What It Measures |
|---|---|
| Total enquiries | Number of responses attributed to the campaign |
| Qualified leads | Enquiries that meet agreed buyer criteria |
| Site visits | Prospects who visit the property |
| Cost per lead | Campaign cost divided by attributed leads |
| Lead-to-site-visit rate | Site visits divided by qualified leads |

No campaign results are claimed because this is a planning exercise, not a live campaign.

## 6. Next Steps

1. Confirm the client's actual objectives and target audience.
2. Validate the campaign budget and preferred timeline.
3. Request current publisher rates and edition availability.
4. Develop a tailored proposal with clear assumptions.
5. Agree on response tracking and reporting.
