# Newspaper Advertising Sales Strategy
### Independent Portfolio Project | Advertising Sales & Marketing

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates how I would approach newspaper advertising sales for a fictional real estate advertiser in Bengaluru. It covers prospect research, client discovery, proposal planning, sales outreach, and campaign performance tracking.

## Business Scenario

GreenNest Developers is a fictional residential real estate company. The proposed campaign aims to generate relevant property enquiries and encourage site visits through a planned newspaper advertising campaign.

## Project Objectives

- Identify and prioritize potential advertising prospects.
- Understand the advertiser's marketing goals and target audience.
- Develop a sample newspaper advertising proposal.
- Prepare a professional sales pitch and outreach approach.
- Define KPIs for tracking campaign performance.

## Project Deliverables

| Deliverable | What It Demonstrates |
|---|---|
| Client Research | Understanding the business and its objectives |
| Prospecting Strategy | Lead identification and qualification |
| Client Discovery | Asking the right questions before pitching |
| Advertising Proposal | Media planning and budget allocation |
| Sales Pitch & Outreach | Communication and objection handling |
| Campaign Tracking | KPI calculations and performance analysis |

## Skills Demonstrated

- Market and prospect research
- B2B sales planning
- Client communication
- Consultative selling
- Advertising proposal development
- Budget allocation
- Campaign KPI tracking
- Basic data analysis

## Key Learning

This project helped me understand how advertising sales combines market research, client relationship management, persuasive communication, and performance measurement.
