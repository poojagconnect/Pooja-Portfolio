# Client Research Sheet

## 1. Project Context

**Project:** Newspaper Advertising Sales Strategy  
**Target Market:** Bengaluru, Karnataka  
**Industry:** Real Estate  
**Purpose:** Identify a potential advertiser and understand its advertising needs.

## 2. Sample Client Profile

**Company Name:** GreenNest Developers  
**Business Type:** Residential real estate developer  
**Market:** Bengaluru  
**Business Scenario:** The company is preparing to launch a residential apartment project and wants to generate awareness and enquiries.

**Important:** GreenNest Developers is a fictional company created for this portfolio project.

## 3. Potential Client Needs

The client may want to:

- Increase awareness of its residential project.
- Reach prospective homebuyers in relevant locations.
- Promote a property launch or open-house event.
- Encourage enquiries and site-visit registrations.
- Strengthen its brand presence in the local market.

These are initial hypotheses to validate with the client, not confirmed requirements.

## 4. Target Audience

**Primary audience:** Prospective homebuyers considering apartments in Bengaluru.

**Possible audience characteristics:**
- People planning to purchase a home.
- Families exploring residential options.
- Buyers interested in specific Bengaluru neighbourhoods.
- People considering attending a property launch or site visit.

The final audience should be refined based on the actual property location, price range, and buyer profile.

## 5. Questions to Ask the Client

1. What is the main objective of your upcoming campaign?
2. Which locations are you targeting?
3. Who is your ideal homebuyer?
4. What makes your project different from competing properties?
5. What is your advertising budget?
6. Which marketing channels are you currently using?
7. How will you measure campaign success?

## 6. Advertising Opportunity

A newspaper display advertisement could be considered if the client wants to communicate project details, build awareness, and promote a launch event.

The recommendation should depend on the client's target audience, budget, campaign timing, and available advertising options.

## 7. Information Still to Validate

- Actual campaign dates
- Confirmed advertising budget
- Property details and approved claims
- Preferred newspaper edition
- Available advertising formats and rates
- Enquiry and site-visit targets

## 8. Sales Executive's Next Action

Arrange a discovery conversation, validate the client's needs, and use the information gathered to prepare a tailored advertising proposal.

## Key Learning

Effective advertising sales begins with understanding the client's business problem rather than immediately trying to sell an advertising package.
