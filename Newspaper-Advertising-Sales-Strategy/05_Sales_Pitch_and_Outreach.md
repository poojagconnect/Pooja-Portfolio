# Sales Pitch & Client Outreach Strategy

## 1. Objective

Develop a professional outreach approach for introducing a newspaper advertising opportunity to a potential real estate advertiser.

This is a simulated sales exercise for my BCCL STEP portfolio project.

## 2. Sample Prospect

- **Company:** GreenNest Developers (fictional)
- **Industry:** Residential real estate
- **Objective:** Generate relevant property enquiries and site visits
- **Proposed solution:** A targeted newspaper display advertising campaign, subject to audience fit and budget validation

## 3. Sample Outreach Email

**Subject: Advertising Opportunity to Reach Prospective Homebuyers**

Dear Marketing Team,

I am reaching out to understand your upcoming property marketing objectives and explore whether newspaper advertising could support your campaign.

A well-planned print advertisement can help communicate project highlights, location advantages, and enquiry details to relevant readers. The appropriate newspaper edition and advertising format would depend on your target audience, campaign timeline, and budget.

Would you be available for a brief discussion to understand your requirements and assess whether a suitable advertising proposal could be developed?

Thank you for your time. I look forward to hearing from you.

Best regards,  
Advertising Sales Representative

## 4. Sample 30-Second Sales Pitch

"Hello, thank you for your time. I would like to understand your current property marketing priorities and the audience you are looking to reach. Depending on your target locations and campaign objectives, newspaper advertising may offer an opportunity to communicate your project's key benefits to relevant readers. If you could share your goals, timeline, and approximate budget, I can explore a suitable proposal for your consideration."

## 5. Discovery Questions During the Meeting

1. Which project or property are you currently promoting?
2. Which locations and buyer segments are most important?
3. What is the main objective: awareness, enquiries, or site visits?
4. What campaign timeline and budget range are you considering?
5. How do you currently measure marketing performance?

## 6. Handling Common Objections

### Objection 1: "Newspaper advertising is expensive."

**Response:**  
"I understand budget efficiency is important. Could we first identify your target audience and campaign objective? We can then explore suitable formats and editions, subject to available inventory and actual rates."

### Objection 2: "We already advertise digitally."

**Response:**  
"That makes sense. Rather than replacing your digital campaigns, we could assess whether print may complement your existing activity. We would need to evaluate audience fit and agree on a way to track responses."

### Objection 3: "We are not planning a campaign right now."

**Response:**  
"Thank you for letting me know. Would it be appropriate to reconnect closer to your next launch or marketing planning cycle?"

## 7. Follow-Up Plan

| Timing | Action |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | Send a personalized introduction |
| Day 3–4 | Follow up politely if there is no response |
| After a meeting | Send a summary of the client's requirements |
| After proposal submission | Confirm receipt and ask for feedback |
| Later, if needed | Follow up according to the client's preferred timeline |

Follow-up timing should be adapted to the client's response and preferences.

## 8. Sales Success Measures

- Prospects contacted
- Response rate
- Meetings scheduled
- Proposals requested
- Proposals accepted
- Revenue generated, when an actual sale occurs

These metrics would be used to assess a real sales process. No actual outreach or sales results are claimed in this portfolio project.

## 9. Learning Outcome

This exercise demonstrates professional communication, consultative selling, objection handling, follow-up planning, and a client-focused approach to advertising sales.

## Disclaimer

GreenNest Developers is fictional. This is an independent portfolio exercise and does not represent actual client communication, a confirmed advertising opportunity, or an official BCCL sales pitch.
