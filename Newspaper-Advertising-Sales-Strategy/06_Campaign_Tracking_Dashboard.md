# Campaign Tracking Dashboard

## 1. Objective

Create a simple performance-tracking framework for a simulated newspaper advertising campaign for GreenNest Developers, a fictional Bengaluru real estate company.

The purpose is to monitor campaign spending, enquiries, lead quality, and site visits.

## 2. Sample Campaign Data

**All figures below are hypothetical and created for portfolio demonstration.**

| Metric | Sample Value |
|---|---:|
| Campaign budget | ₹2,00,000 |
| Campaign spend | ₹1,80,000 |
| Total enquiries | 120 |
| Qualified leads | 60 |
| Site visits booked | 30 |
| Site visits completed | 20 |

## 3. Performance Calculations

### Budget Utilization

Formula: Campaign spend ÷ Campaign budget × 100

Calculation: ₹1,80,000 ÷ ₹2,00,000 × 100

**Result: 90%**

### Cost per Enquiry

Formula: Campaign spend ÷ Total enquiries

Calculation: ₹1,80,000 ÷ 120

**Result: ₹1,500 per enquiry**

### Cost per Qualified Lead

Formula: Campaign spend ÷ Qualified leads

Calculation: ₹1,80,000 ÷ 60

**Result: ₹3,000 per qualified lead**

### Site Visit Completion Rate

Formula: Completed site visits ÷ Site visits booked × 100

Calculation: 20 ÷ 30 × 100

**Result: 66.7%**

## 4. Key Observations

Based on this hypothetical dataset:

- The campaign used 90% of the allocated budget.
- Half of all enquiries qualified as leads under the assumed criteria.
- Two-thirds of booked site visits were completed.
- The next review should investigate why some enquiries did not qualify and why some booked visits did not take place.

These observations describe the sample data only; they are not actual campaign results.

## 5. Recommended Dashboard Visuals

If recreating this project in Power BI or Excel, include:

1. KPI cards for campaign spend, enquiries, qualified leads, and completed site visits.
2. A funnel showing enquiries → qualified leads → booked visits → completed visits.
3. A budget-versus-spend comparison.
4. A bar chart comparing cost per enquiry and cost per qualified lead.

## 6. Suggested Actions

- Review lead quality with the advertiser.
- Check whether the advertisement reached the intended audience.
- Improve response tracking and follow-up.
- Compare results against agreed campaign targets before making further spending recommendations.

## 7. Learning Outcome

This exercise demonstrates basic campaign reporting, KPI calculations, funnel analysis, and data-informed sales recommendations.

## Disclaimer

This is an independent portfolio exercise. GreenNest Developers and all performance figures are fictional. The results do not represent actual BCCL campaigns or advertising performance.
