# Newspaper Advertising Sales Strategy

## Project Overview
This is an independent marketing and sales portfolio project designed to demonstrate how a newspaper advertising sales executive can identify potential clients, understand their business needs, prepare advertising proposals, and develop strategies to generate advertising revenue.

## Business Scenario
The project uses a hypothetical real-estate developer in Bengaluru planning to promote a residential property launch through newspaper advertising.

**Note:** This is a simulated case study and is not an official BCCL project.

## Project Objectives
- Identify potential advertisers and understand their business objectives.
- Research target audiences and advertising opportunities.
- Develop a newspaper advertising strategy.
- Prepare a sample client pitch and advertising proposal.
- Build a prospecting and follow-up plan.
- Define key performance indicators to evaluate campaign performance.

## Target Client
**Industry:** Real Estate  
**Location:** Bengaluru, Karnataka  
**Business Objective:** Increase awareness of a residential property launch and encourage enquiries from prospective homebuyers.

The client profile and campaign scenario are hypothetical.

## Project Approach
1. Client and market research
2. Identification of advertising opportunities
3. Target audience analysis
4. Newspaper advertising strategy
5. Sample proposal and budget planning
6. Client pitching and objection handling
7. Lead tracking and follow-up
8. Campaign performance measurement

## Key Skills Demonstrated
- Business development
- Client research and prospecting
- Marketing strategy
- Sales presentation
- Negotiation planning
- Revenue-oriented thinking
- Basic data analysis

## Tools
- GitHub for project documentation
- PowerPoint or Canva for presentations
- Excel or Google Sheets for prospect tracking and budget planning

## Expected Outcome
A structured advertising sales case study demonstrating the process of identifying potential advertisers, developing a suitable proposal, and planning for sustainable client relationships.

This is an independent portfolio project using hypothetical business details and illustrative assumptions. It does not represent actual BCCL client data, advertising rates, or campaign results.
