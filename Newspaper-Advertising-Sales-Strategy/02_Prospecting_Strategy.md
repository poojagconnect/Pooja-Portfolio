# Prospecting Strategy — Newspaper Advertising Sales

## 1. Objective
Identify and prioritize potential real-estate advertisers in Bengaluru for a simulated newspaper advertising sales campaign.

## 2. Prospect List

| Company | Research source | Potential advertising opportunity | Next action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Concorde | https://www.concorde.in/ | Research residential launches and campaign needs | Review project announcements |

| Vajram Group | https://www.vajramgroup.com/ | Investigate possible launch and property advertising needs | Review featured projects |

**Note:** These companies are research prospects only. Their interest in newspaper advertising has not been established.

## 3. Prospect Qualification Criteria

Before contacting a company, assess:

- Does it have a relevant residential project?
- Is there evidence of an upcoming launch or marketing activity?
- Does the proposed campaign fit the company's target audience?
- Can the relevant marketing decision-maker be identified?
- Is there a plausible reason to consider newspaper advertising?

## 4. Prioritization Method

Assign each prospect a score from 1 to 5 on the following criteria:

- Evidence of a relevant campaign opportunity
- Fit with the proposed audience
- Availability of public project information
- Ability to identify a relevant decision-maker

Use the total score to decide which prospects deserve further research. Record the evidence behind each score instead of guessing.

## 5. Outreach Plan

1. Research the company and its projects.
2. Identify the appropriate marketing contact through legitimate public business channels.
3. Prepare a short, personalized introduction.
4. Ask about upcoming campaigns and advertising objectives.
5. Record the response and schedule a suitable follow-up.
6. Prepare a tailored proposal only after understanding the client's requirements.

## 6. Sales Tracking

Track the following fields:

Company | Contact Role | Research Date | Opportunity | Status | Next Action | Follow-up Date

Suggested statuses: Researching, Ready to Contact, Contacted, Meeting Scheduled, Proposal Requested, Won, Lost.

## 7. Learning Outcome

This exercise demonstrates structured prospecting, market research, opportunity qualification, and follow-up planning for a newspaper advertising sales role.

## Disclaimer
This is an independent portfolio exercise. No listed company is represented as an actual client, interested advertiser, or confirmed sales opportunity.
