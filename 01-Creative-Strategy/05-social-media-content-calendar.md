# NIVEA Aloe Protection Family Care Campaign
## Social Media Content Calendar

### Campaign Overview

**Brand:** NIVEA  
**Product:** Aloe Protection Body Lotion  
**Campaign Theme:** One Lotion. Multiple Solutions.  
**Campaign Objective:** Build product awareness, encourage audience engagement, and communicate the product's moisturising and sun-protection positioning.

### Target Platforms

- Instagram
- Facebook
- YouTube Shorts

### 7-Day Content Calendar

| Day | Content Format | Content Idea | Objective |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Instagram poster | Introduce the campaign with the message "One Lotion. Multiple Solutions." | Awareness |
| Day 2 | Instagram carousel | Share the daily skincare routine for busy adults. | Education |
| Day 3 | Instagram Reel | Show a simple body-moisturising routine. | Engagement |
| Day 4 | Instagram Story | Run a poll: "What matters most in your daily body-care routine?" | Interaction |
| Day 5 | Family-focused creative | Share a family-care moment featuring the product. | Brand connection |
| Day 6 | Skincare tips carousel | Share practical tips for maintaining moisturised skin. | Education |
| Day 7 | Campaign recap post | Summarise the campaign message and invite audiences to explore the product. | Consideration |

### Sample Social Media Caption

Everyday care deserves a place in your routine. 💙

Discover a simple way to care for your skin with NIVEA Aloe Protection Body Lotion.

Make moisturising part of your everyday self-care ritual.

**One Lotion. Multiple Solutions.**

#NIVEA #BodyCare #DailySkincare #SkinMoisture

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Reach and impressions
- Likes, comments, shares, and saves
- Engagement rate
- Video views and completion rate
- Link clicks and product-page visits

### Measurement Approach

Review campaign performance weekly. Compare content formats to identify which posts generate stronger engagement and audience interest. Use the findings to improve future creative concepts and posting schedules.

### Important Note

Confirm all product benefits and sun-protection claims against the current product packaging and approved brand information before publishing campaign content.
