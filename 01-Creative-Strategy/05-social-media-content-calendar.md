### 5. Creative Content Approach

The content calendar uses four creative approaches:

- **Everyday storytelling:** Connect body care with familiar daily routines.
- **Educational content:** Share general body-care tips without making unsupported product claims.
- **Family-care messaging:** Present relatable family-care moments while avoiding unverified claims about suitability for specific age groups.
- **Product-focused communication:** Encourage audiences to explore the product using verified information and an approved destination link.
