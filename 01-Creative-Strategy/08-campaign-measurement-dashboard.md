---

### 4. KPI Calculation

Using the illustrative values above:

**Click-Through Rate (CTR)**

CTR = (Link Clicks ÷ Impressions) × 100

= (800 ÷ 40,000) × 100

= 2.00%

**Cost Per Click (CPC)**

CPC = Advertising Spend ÷ Link Clicks

= ₹7,000 ÷ 800

= ₹8.75 per link click

**Frequency**

Frequency = Impressions ÷ Reach

= 40,000 ÷ 25,000

= 1.6 impressions per person on average

**Engagement Rate by Reach**

Engagement Rate = (Engagements ÷ Reach) × 100

= (1,200 ÷ 25,000) × 100

= 4.8%

These calculations demonstrate how campaign reporting can turn advertising data into measurable indicators.

### 7. Reporting Schedule

**Daily Monitoring**
- Check ad delivery and spending.
- Confirm destination links are working.
- Monitor unusual changes in results.

**Mid-Campaign Review**
- Compare audience and creative performance.
- Review CTR and CPC.
- Check landing-page views and traffic quality.
- Recommend adjustments where supported by data.

**Final Campaign Report**
- Summarise the final spend and results.
- Compare actual outcomes with campaign objectives.
- Identify the strongest-performing creative and platform.
- Document lessons and recommendations for the next campaign.

---

### 8. Optimisation Recommendations

Use the following decision framework when actual results become available.

**If CTR is low:**
- Review the headline and main message.
- Test a different visual or opening line.
- Check whether the creative is relevant to the audience.

**If CPC is high:**
- Compare audience and placement performance.
- Review creative relevance and click quality.
- Test alternative campaign configurations.

**If engagement is strong but website traffic is weak:**
- Make the call to action clearer.
- Check whether the advertisement matches the landing page.
- Review link placement and destination-page usability.

**If traffic is strong but conversions are weak:**
- Check page loading speed and mobile usability.
- Review product information and the purchase journey.
- Verify analytics and conversion tracking before drawing conclusions.

These are potential actions, not conclusions about actual campaign performance.
