### 4. KPI Calculations

**Budget utilisation**

Budget utilisation = (Final spend ÷ Planned budget) × 100

= (₹14,000 ÷ ₹14,000) × 100

= **100%**

**Budget remaining**

Budget remaining = Planned budget − Final spend

= ₹14,000 − ₹14,000

= **₹0**

**Click-through rate (CTR)**

CTR = (Link clicks ÷ Impressions) × 100

= (1,600 ÷ 80,000) × 100

= **2.00%**

**Cost per click (CPC)**

CPC = Final spend ÷ Link clicks

= ₹14,000 ÷ 1,600

= **₹8.75**

**Cost per thousand impressions (CPM)**

CPM = (Final spend ÷ Impressions) × 1,000

= (₹14,000 ÷ 80,000) × 1,000

= **₹175.00**

**Average frequency**

Frequency = Impressions ÷ Reach

= 80,000 ÷ 50,000

= **1.60**

**Engagement rate by reach**

Engagement rate = (Engagements ÷ Reach) × 100

= (2,400 ÷ 50,000) × 100

= **4.80%**

**Click-to-page-view rate**

Click-to-page-view rate = (Landing-page views ÷ Link clicks) × 100

= (1,200 ÷ 1,600) × 100

= **75.00%**

All calculations above are based on simulated figures and are internally consistent.
