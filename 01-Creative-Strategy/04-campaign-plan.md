# Digital Campaign Plan — NIVEA Body Lotion

## 1. Campaign Overview

**Campaign Name:** Make Everyday Care Your Moment  
**Brand:** NIVEA  
**Product:** Body lotion  
**Campaign Type:** Proposed digital advertising campaign  
**Duration:** 4 weeks  
**Primary Platforms:** Instagram and Facebook

## 2. Campaign Objective

Build awareness of the selected body lotion and encourage the target audience to explore the product.

### Proposed Objectives

- Introduce the campaign message to the intended audience.
- Create relatable skincare content.
- Encourage meaningful engagement with campaign creatives.
- Drive visits to the relevant product page.

These are proposed objectives, not achieved results.

## 3. Target Audience

Young adults aged 20–35 interested in personal care and everyday skincare.

This is a proposed audience segment for the portfolio exercise. It should be validated through research.

## 4. Campaign Content Plan

| Week | Content Idea | Format | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | Introduce the campaign theme | Reel | Awareness |
| Week 2 | Share an everyday skincare routine | Carousel | Engagement |
| Week 3 | Present the self-care creative concept | Short video | Brand connection |
| Week 4 | Feature the product and a clear call to action | Story | Product-page visits |

## 5. Creative Strategy

The campaign will use three creative approaches:

- **Relatable storytelling:** Connect the product to everyday routines.
- **Routine-based communication:** Present body care as a simple habit.
- **Emotional storytelling:** Focus on taking a moment for personal care.

All product claims and visuals should be checked against official product information before publication.

## 6. Performance Measurement

| Metric | What It Measures |
|---|---|
| Reach | Number of unique accounts exposed to the campaign |
| Impressions | Total number of times the content is displayed |
| Engagement rate | Engagement relative to the selected reach or impression base |
| Click-through rate | Percentage of impressions that result in clicks |
| Landing page visits | Visits to the product page attributed to the campaign |

### Example Formulas

**Engagement rate by reach**

Engagements ÷ Reach × 100

**Click-through rate**

Link clicks ÷ Impressions × 100

Actual results would need to be collected from campaign analytics.

## 7. A/B Testing Plan

Test two versions of an advertisement while keeping other conditions as similar as possible.

**Version A:** "Make Time for Everyday Care."

**Version B:** "Pause. Care. Continue."

Compare their click-through rates and engagement, ensuring that the test has enough data to support a useful conclusion.

## 8. Reporting and Optimization

At the end of the campaign, review the results by creative concept, audience, and platform. Identify which messages performed better, investigate possible reasons, and recommend improvements for the next campaign.

## 9. Project Disclosure

This is an independent campaign-planning exercise. No advertisements have been published, no media budget has been spent, and no real campaign performance results are claimed.
