**Campaign End Date:** To be finalized

*Portfolio note: This is an independent campaign-planning exercise. The campaign has not been launched, and the proposed budget has not been spent.*

## 2. Campaign Objectives

The campaign focuses on four proposed objectives:

- **Brand Awareness:** Introduce NIVEA Aloe Protection Body Lotion to relevant audiences.
- **Engagement:** Encourage likes, comments, shares, saves, and Story interactions.
- **Product Consideration:** Encourage audiences to learn more about the product.
- **Website Traffic:** Direct interested audiences to an approved product page or retailer website.

These objectives describe the intended outcomes of the proposed campaign, not achieved results.

## 3. Target Audience

### Primary Audience

- Adults aged 25–44 interested in skincare, body care, and personal care.
- Working professionals and busy adults looking for convenient everyday skincare routines.
- Adults who purchase personal-care products for their households.

### Secondary Audience

- Adults aged 18–24 interested in beauty and skincare content.
- Adults researching body lotions and moisturising products.
- Family-oriented shoppers interested in everyday personal-care products.

### Geographic Targeting

The proposed market is India, beginning with selected cities based on product availability and campaign objectives.

### Targeting Approach

- Test broad adult audiences against relevant interest-based audiences.
- Use permitted skincare and personal-care interests where available.
- Consider consent-based website retargeting only when applicable privacy and tracking requirements are met.
- Review audience and placement performance before recommending changes.

Audience settings should follow current advertising-platform policies and applicable privacy requirements.

## 7. Performance Measurement

The following metrics would be used to evaluate the campaign if it were launched.

| Metric | What It Measures |
|---|---|
| Reach | Number of unique accounts exposed to the campaign |
| Impressions | Total number of times advertisements are displayed |
| Engagement rate | Engagement relative to the chosen reach or impression base |
| Click-through rate (CTR) | Percentage of impressions resulting in link clicks |
| Landing-page visits | Visits attributed to campaign links |
| Cost per click (CPC) | Media cost divided by link clicks |
| Cost per thousand impressions (CPM) | Media cost per 1,000 impressions |

### Example Formulas

**Engagement rate by reach**

Engagements ÷ Reach × 100

**Click-through rate**

Link clicks ÷ Impressions × 100

**Cost per click**

Media spend ÷ Link clicks

Actual performance values would be collected from advertising-platform analytics and relevant website analytics if the campaign were implemented. No actual results are claimed in this portfolio project.

## 8. A/B Testing Plan

The proposed test will compare two campaign headlines while keeping other variables as similar as possible.

**Version A:** “One Lotion. Multiple Solutions.”

**Version B:** “Care for Your Skin Every Day.”

The test would compare click-through rate and engagement, using sufficient data before drawing conclusions. Audience, placement, creative format, and budget conditions should be controlled as far as practical.

The winning version would be selected based on the campaign objective and the quality of the available data.

- Identifying potential improvements to messaging, placements, and budget allocation.
