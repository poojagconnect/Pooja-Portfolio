# Target Audience and Consumer Insights

## 1. Target Audience

**Primary audience:** Young adults aged 20–35 who are interested in personal care and everyday skincare.

**Lifestyle:** Students, young professionals, and working adults who want a simple skincare routine.

**Potential needs:**
- Convenient everyday skincare.
- Skin that feels soft and moisturized.
- Products that fit their budget and routine.
- Clear product information before purchasing.

*Note: This audience is a proposed campaign segment, not a verified description of NIVEA's actual customer base.*

## 2. Customer Persona

**Name:** Ananya (fictional persona)  
**Age:** 25  
**Occupation:** Young professional  
**Shopping behaviour:** Discovers personal care products through social media, online reviews, and retail stores.

**Needs:** An easy-to-use body lotion for her everyday routine.

**Potential barrier:** She may ignore advertisements that feel repetitive, overly promotional, or irrelevant.

**Advertising opportunity:** Show how body lotion can fit naturally into an everyday self-care routine.

## 3. Consumer Insight Hypothesis

"Busy young adults may be more interested in skincare when the product is presented as a simple, enjoyable part of their daily routine rather than another task."

This is a hypothesis to validate through consumer research, not a confirmed research finding.

## 4. Key Message

**Campaign message:** Make everyday skincare a moment for yourself.

## 5. Creative Direction

The campaign should use relatable everyday situations, clean visuals, concise copy, and a friendly tone. Product claims should be checked against official product information before publication.

## 6. Research Needed

To validate this strategy, review customer feedback, competitor advertisements, and relevant search trends. Record the sources and distinguish actual findings from assumptions.
