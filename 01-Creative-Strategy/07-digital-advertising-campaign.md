# NIVEA Aloe Protection Family Care Campaign
## Digital Advertising Campaign Plan

### 1. Campaign Overview

**Brand:** NIVEA  
**Product:** NIVEA Aloe Protection Body Lotion SPF 15  
**Campaign Theme:** One Lotion. Multiple Solutions.  
**Campaign Type:** Digital Advertising  
**Proposed Platforms:** Instagram and Facebook  
**Campaign Duration:** 14 days  
**Market:** India (proposed)  

This campaign is designed to introduce the product to relevant audiences, communicate its everyday body-care benefits, and encourage people to explore the product.

*Portfolio note: This is a proposed campaign plan, not a live advertising campaign.*

---

### 2. Campaign Objectives

The campaign will focus on four objectives:

1. **Brand Awareness:** Increase awareness of NIVEA Aloe Protection Body Lotion among relevant audiences.
2. **Engagement:** Encourage likes, comments, shares, saves, and Story interactions.
3. **Product Consideration:** Encourage audiences to learn more about the product.
4. **Traffic:** Drive relevant visitors to an approved product page or retailer website.

### 3. Target Audience

**Primary Audience**

- Adults aged 25–44.
- People interested in skincare, body care, and personal care.
- Working professionals and busy adults looking for convenient daily skincare routines.
- Adults who purchase personal-care products for their households.

**Secondary Audience**

- Adults aged 18–24 interested in beauty and skincare content.
- Adults who research body lotions and moisturising products.
- Family-oriented shoppers interested in everyday personal-care products.

**Geographic Targeting**

Start with selected cities in India, depending on distribution and campaign objectives. Expand to additional regions after reviewing performance.

**Targeting Approach**

- Use relevant, platform-permitted skincare and personal-care interests where available.
- Test broad adult audiences against relevant interest-based audiences.
- Use consent-based website retargeting only when the necessary tracking and privacy requirements are met.
- Exclude irrelevant placements or audiences based on observed performance.

Audience settings should follow current advertising-platform policies and applicable privacy requirements.

---

### 4. Campaign Creative Strategy

The campaign will use the existing NIVEA family-care poster as the main visual reference.

**Creative A: Campaign Awareness**

Headline: One Lotion. Multiple Solutions.

Primary message: Make everyday body care a simple part of your routine.

Format: Instagram feed post and Facebook feed ad.

Objective: Reach and awareness.

**Creative B: Everyday Sun Protection**

Headline: Care for Your Skin Every Day.

Primary message: Explore everyday skincare
